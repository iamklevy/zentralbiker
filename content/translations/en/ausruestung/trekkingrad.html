After 23,000 kilometres through America and Asia, we decided on the Aarios "Expedition" frame. Aarios, based in Gretzenbach, designs and builds its frames in-house.

The backbone is the hand-brazed, lugged 25CrMo4 tube set frame. Chromoly steel is very strong and flexible, and hardly heavier than aluminium. This no-compromise frame is built to our needs in terms of geometry, mounting points and cable routing. A powder coating protects the surface against corrosion.

As cycling globetrotters, we want to build up our bikes ourselves. The Shimano 9-speed groupset is replaced by a Rohloff hub gear, and a suspension seatpost will soften bumpy tracks. The SON hub dynamo supplies power for the lights and a USB charging plug. The racks and any connecting parts that could corrode are replaced by stainless steel products.
